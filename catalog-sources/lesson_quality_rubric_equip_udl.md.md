Lesson Quality Rubric — EQuIP + UDL Overlay

Status: Active

Owner: Product Lead (You)

Last updated: 2025-09-18

3 = Meets; 2 = Approaches; 1 = Partial; 0 = Not Evident

1. Alignment & Coherence
3: Objectives, tasks, and assessment align tightly to the standard; canonical ID shown.

2: Minor gaps; mostly aligned.

1: Significant gaps or unclear alignment.

0: Misaligned.

2. Instructional Design (Model Fidelity)
3: Clear I‑We‑You or 5E/PBL structure with checks for understanding.

2: Structure present with occasional drift.

1: Structure weak; minimal checks.

0: No coherent structure.

3. Access & Supports (UDL/HLP)
3: At least one scaffold per flagged student; rationale (HLP/UDL) shown.

2: Supports present but inconsistent or weak rationale.

1: Generic supports; not personalized.

0: No supports.

4. Assessment for Learning
3: Exit slip includes DOK + misconception + 0–3 criteria; drives next steps.

2: Slip present, missing one element.

1: Low‑value prompt; no criteria.

5. Materials & Licensing
3: OER only; TASL printed; accessibility noted.

2: OER with incomplete metadata.

1: Mixed sources; unclear licensing.

0: Unlicensed/unknown sources.

6. Professional Tone & Clarity
3: Clear microcopy; actionable steps; teacher‑ready.

2: Mostly clear; some ambiguity.

1: Vague; requires guessing.

0: Unusable.

#### Pass threshold:

Average ≥ 2.5 and no category at 0.
